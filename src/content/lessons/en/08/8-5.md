## Understand the problem

A Dataset defines how to access an example and its target. A DataLoader organises loading, batching and optionally shuffling. This separation avoids mixing reading logic, augmentation and model computation in one loop.

## How it works

Adapting a pretrained network often involves replacing its classification head. Earlier layers may be frozen or fine-tuned at a smaller learning rate. Freezing parameters and switching to eval mode are separate decisions: BatchNorm can still update statistics in train mode. Creating the optimiser after modifications includes new parameters.

$$
\mathrm{features}(x)\in\mathbb R^{512}\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Linear}(512,7)}\mathbb R^7
$$

## Develop the reasoning

For an indexable Dataset, `__len__` exposes example count and `__getitem__` loads one example. DataLoader applies a sampling policy and then a collation function. Workers can prepare data while the model computes; pinned memory can speed transfers to a GPU. Ordering and transformations must remain appropriate for evaluation. For variable-length sequences, collation must define padding and masks instead of assuming every shape matches.

## Worked example

A feature extractor produces 512 features and its head predicts 1,000 classes. For a 7-class task, a new Linear(512,7) has 512×7+7 = 3,591 parameters. Input transforms must remain compatible with pretraining.

## Understanding check

Why are random training transforms not automatically used for validation?

<details>
<summary>Reveal the worked answer</summary>

They would change the distribution and measurement across evaluations. Validation transforms should be fixed by protocol; any test-time augmentation must be explicitly defined.
